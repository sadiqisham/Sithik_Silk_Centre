Our story · Our promise

A family tradition, stitched with trust.

For over 50 years, SITHIK SILK CENTRE has been part of a family tradition that spans three generations. As a family-run retail store, we continue to serve customers with thoughtful selections, dependable choices and a warm welcome.

Today, our retail collections include clothing for men, women and children, alongside sarees, dress materials and everyday essentials — all available under one roof.
